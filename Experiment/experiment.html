Imagine you are at a party. You walk into the kitchen and overhear somebody ask somebody else a question:


Bill asks: "Does Drew believe that Patty lives in Canada?"


We ask you: Given what Bill says, is he certain that Patty lives in Canada?

You give your response on a sliding scale from 'no' to 'yes'.

There are a total of 16 questions. The task will take about 5 minutes and you will be paid 1.20 $.

We have included some questions for which we already know the answer. If you are paying attention, you are sure to get those right! At the end of the task, we will ask you some short questions about you and your language use. You will be paid no matter what you answer there, so please answer truthfully.

Thank you!
